Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI price prediction work?
Our supervised machine learning model (Random Forest / XGBoost) is trained on domestic highway routes, taking into account road distance, total luggage weight, bag count, category fragility, and transport urgency to predict fair market prices.
Which cities are currently supported?
We support all major domestic corridors in Pakistan including Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Multan, Faisalabad, and Quetta.
Can I track my luggage in real time?
Yes! The tracking portal shows step-by-step milestone progression from "Confirmed" to "Driver Assigned", "Picked Up", "In Transit", and "Delivered".
